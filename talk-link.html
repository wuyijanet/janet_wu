UX Research / App Design 用户研究 / 应用设计

Talk Link

Talk Link is a group UX design project exploring how a community-based app could support immigrants and international students facing language barriers in Canada. Through user research, paper prototyping, and usability testing, we developed a concept connecting language learning with shared interests, conversation, and local cultural activities. Talk Link 是一个小组用户体验设计项目，探索如何通过社区型应用， 支持在加拿大面临语言障碍的移民与国际学生。我们通过用户研究、 纸面原型和可用性测试，将语言学习与兴趣交流、 日常对话及本地文化活动相结合。

Design Challenge 设计挑战

Language barriers can affect more than everyday communication. They can also make it harder to build confidence, form local connections, and feel a sense of belonging. Our design question was: how might we help newcomers practise a language while finding accessible ways to participate in their community? 语言障碍不仅影响日常沟通，也可能削弱表达信心， 增加建立本地人际关系和获得归属感的难度。 因此，我们关注的问题是：如何让新来者在练习语言的同时， 更容易找到参与社区生活的途径？

User Research & Insights 用户研究与洞察

We collected 16 questionnaire responses and conducted an online interview with a Chinese immigrant in Canada. Using qualitative coding and empathy mapping, we explored participants’ experiences of communication, language learning, and cultural adjustment. 我们收集了 16 份问卷回复，并对一位在加拿大的华人移民 进行了线上访谈。通过定性编码与同理心地图， 分析参与者在日常沟通、语言学习和文化适应中的经历。

  • Confidence: Anxiety and fear of making mistakes can discourage participation. 表达信心： 焦虑和对犯错的担忧可能降低主动交流的意愿。
  • Community access: Learners need opportunities to connect with others beyond formal language study. 社区参与： 学习者需要课堂之外与他人建立联系的机会。
  • Relevant learning: Shared interests and everyday situations can provide meaningful contexts for language practice. 情境化学习： 共同兴趣与日常场景可以为语言练习提供更具体的语境。

Prototype Concept 原型设计

We developed a paper prototype of a social app that brings language practice into community interaction. The concept includes four connected areas: 我们制作了社区交流应用的纸面原型， 将语言练习融入人与人的互动，主要包含四个相互连接的部分：

  • Interest Forums: Explore topics such as food, gaming, sports, and music; create posts and join discussions. 兴趣论坛： 围绕美食、游戏、运动和音乐等话题浏览内容、发帖与交流。
  • Vocabulary Support: Open marked words within posts to view their meaning and pronunciation information. 词汇辅助： 点击帖子中标记的词汇，查看释义与发音信息。
  • Video Conversations: Select a practice language and review a potential conversation partner before accepting a call. 视频交流： 选择希望练习的语言，查看交流对象的信息后决定是否通话。
  • Cultural Calendar: Discover holidays and local activities through event information, introductory videos, and location guidance. 文化日历： 通过活动介绍、文化视频和地点指引， 了解节日与本地社区活动。

The prototype demonstrates proposed user flows rather than a fully developed application. 该原型用于展示功能构想与操作流程，尚未开发为完整应用。

User Testing & Refinement 用户测试与迭代

We conducted two usability testing sessions with one international student and one local Canadian student. Participants explored the prototype and completed tasks such as finding their profile and entering an interest forum. We observed their navigation and collected feedback through follow-up discussion. 我们分别邀请一位国际学生和一位加拿大本地学生， 开展了两次可用性测试。参与者体验原型，并完成查找个人资料、 进入兴趣论坛等任务。我们观察操作过程， 并通过后续讨论收集反馈。

  • Broader language support: Extend beyond the initial Chinese–English experience to accommodate more linguistic backgrounds. 拓展语言支持： 在初始中英双语体验之外，考虑更多语言背景的用户。
  • Interactive learning: Add language and cultural games to make practice more engaging. 增加互动学习： 加入语言与文化小游戏，提高练习过程的参与感。

These sessions informed our design revisions. Further testing with a larger and more diverse group would be needed to evaluate the concept’s effectiveness. 这些反馈为原型修改提供了方向。 后续仍需邀请更多不同背景的用户， 进一步验证设计的可用性与实际效果。

Project Reflection 项目反思

This project helped us move from a broad language-learning challenge to a community-focused design concept. Our own experiences as international students informed the initial direction, while research and testing challenged us to consider needs beyond our own. A key learning was to design opportunities for connection alongside tools for language practice. 这个项目让我们从宽泛的语言学习问题， 逐步形成以社区交流为核心的设计方向。 国际学生的自身经历帮助我们理解问题， 而研究与测试也促使我们关注个人经验之外的需求。 我们的重要收获是：除了提供语言练习工具， 设计也可以为人与人建立联系创造机会。
