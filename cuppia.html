Digital Marketing / Campaign Design

Cuppia Campaign

This project is a digital marketing campaign created for Cuppia, a mug brand focused on seasonal and limited-edition collections. The campaign includes an email advertisement, Instagram promotional visuals, a landing page design, presentation slides, and a project explanation video. The project explores visual consistency, brand storytelling, and multi-platform campaign communication.

Landing Page Design

The landing page was designed as the main destination for the campaign, where users can view the product, understand the promotion, and take further action. The layout focuses on clarity, visual appeal, and user flow.

Project Highlights

  • Developed a multi-platform campaign system with a consistent visual identity.
  • Designed campaign materials for both social media and direct marketing channels.
  • Combined branding, promotion, and user engagement into one integrated project.
  • Created a clear visual direction that supports product storytelling and seasonal marketing.
  • Strengthened the campaign presentation through both static visuals and video explanation.

Development Process

The project began with defining the campaign theme, target audience, and overall brand tone. In the early stage, I focused on building a clear visual direction that could be applied consistently across different formats, including social media graphics, email advertising, landing page design, presentation slides, and video explanation.

After establishing the campaign concept, I developed each asset based on its platform and communication purpose. The Instagram content was designed to attract attention quickly, the email ad focused on promotional messaging, and the landing page was structured to present information more clearly and support user action. The final outcome is a campaign system that combines visual consistency with platform-specific communication.
