Graphic Design / Poster Design

Floral Dreams Poster

Floral Dreams is a promotional poster designed for the grand opening of a flower shop.The project focuses on creating a visually appealing advertisement that communicates promotional information clearly while conveying a warm, welcoming, and elegant brand image. Through thoughtful layout, typography, color selection, and visual hierarchy, the poster aims to attract potential customers and increase awareness of the new store.

Poster Preview

This project presents a promotional poster designed for the flower shop "Floral Dreams". The poster was created to attract customers, communicate key promotional information, and establish a warm and inviting visual identity for the brand.

Design Essay

This essay explains the design decisions behind the poster, including layout structure, image selection, color palette, typography, and the application of Gestalt design principles used to improve visual communication and audience engagement.

Tools

  • Adobe InDesign
  • Adobe Photoshop

Project Details

  • Type: Promotional Poster
  • Role: Graphic Designer
  • Theme: Flower Shop Opening
  • Focus: Visual Communication, Customer Attraction

Project Highlights

  • Designed a promotional poster for a fictional flower shop called Floral Dreams.
  • Applied Gestalt design principles to improve visual hierarchy and readability.
  • Used a balanced image-and-text layout to communicate information clearly.
  • Selected warm color tones to create a welcoming and positive atmosphere.
  • Designed typography to emphasize key promotional information and attract customer attention.

Design Process

This poster was designed to promote the grand opening of a flower shop called Floral Dreams. The goal was to create a warm and inviting visual identity while clearly communicating promotional information to potential customers.

  • Balanced image-and-text layout
  • Warm orange color palette
  • Gestalt principles (proximity and continuity)
  • Clear visual hierarchy and typography
  • Focus on customer attraction and brand awareness
