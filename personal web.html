Development Process

  • The project started by defining the purpose and target audience, aiming to present personal achievements and work clearly to professors, peers, and potential employers.
  • I then designed the information architecture, organizing the website into multiple pages including Home, CV, Portfolio, Research, and Photography.
  • Wireframes were created in Figma for both desktop and mobile layouts. After that, I implemented the website using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap to ensure responsiveness.
  • Finally, I refined the design through testing, improving layout, usability, and consistency across different devices and browsers.
