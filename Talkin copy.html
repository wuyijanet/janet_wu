Second World 第二世界

Second World is an original AI-generated science fiction short film series set in a parallel world formed by abandoned possibilities and forgotten futures. The story follows Lin Xia, who accidentally enters the Second World, and Lu Chuan, who has been trapped there for ten years. Together, they uncover the rules of this world and search for a way to return to reality. The project covers the complete production process, including story development, scriptwriting, storyboard design, character creation, AI visual generation, video production, and editing. 《第二世界》是一部原创AI科幻悬疑短剧，故事发生在一个由被放弃的可能性与未被选择的未来共同构成的平行世界。女主角林夏意外进入第二世界，并遇见已经被困在这里十年的陆川。两人在寻找返回现实方法的过程中，逐渐发现这个世界的运行规则与隐藏真相。项目涵盖世界观构建、剧本创作、分镜设计、角色设定、AI画面生成、AI视频制作及后期剪辑等完整制作流程。

Project Preview 项目预览

The series combines cinematic storytelling with AI-assisted image and video generation. The visual direction uses a realistic science fiction style, contrasting cold futuristic environments with warmer character-focused scenes. 该系列结合电影化叙事与AI图像、视频生成技术，整体采用写实科幻视觉风格，通过冷色未来城市与暖色人物场景之间的对比，建立第二世界独特的空间氛围。

Episodes 剧集展示

The project is currently completed through Episode 4. Each episode continues the central storyline while gradually revealing the characters, rules, and hidden structure of the Second World. 项目目前已完成至第四集。四集内容围绕同一条主线展开，通过连续剧情逐步介绍主要人物、世界规则及第二世界背后的秘密。

Episode 01 · Terminal Station 第一集 · 终点站

Lin Xia wakes up alone on a train and arrives at an unknown terminal called the Second World. 林夏在一列空无一人的列车上醒来，并抵达名为“第二世界”的陌生终点站。

Episode 02 · Echo 第二集 · 回声

After being followed by mysterious shadow figures, Lin Xia encounters Lu Chuan for the first time. 林夏被神秘黑影追逐，并在逃离过程中第一次遇见陆川。

Episode 03 · The One Who Waited 第三集 · 等待的人

Lu Chuan brings Lin Xia into an abandoned library and reveals that he arrived in the same way ten years earlier. 陆川将林夏带入废弃图书馆，并告诉她，十年前的自己也以相同方式来到这里。

Episode 04 · The Rules 第四集 · 规则

Lin Xia learns the rules required to survive in the Second World and discovers a hidden room beneath the library. 林夏了解到在第二世界生存必须遵守的规则，并在图书馆中发现隐藏密室。

Production Workflow 制作流程

The project follows a complete AI-assisted film production workflow. Each stage was developed around the narrative and visual requirements of the series, allowing the story, characters, environments, and final videos to maintain a consistent direction. 项目采用完整的AI辅助影视制作流程。每个制作阶段都围绕连续剧情与统一视觉方向展开，使剧本、人物、场景与最终视频能够保持一致。

01

Concept Development 创意构思

Developed the central concept of a parallel world formed by abandoned futures and unrealized possibilities. 构建由“未被选择的未来”和“被放弃的可能性”形成的平行世界概念。

02

Scriptwriting 剧本创作

Divided the main storyline into connected episodes and designed the dialogue, suspense, and narrative rhythm. 将整体故事拆分为连续剧集，并设计对白、悬念节点与每集叙事节奏。

03

Storyboard Design 分镜设计

Planned character positions, shot sizes, camera movement, lighting, and transitions before generating the scenes. 在画面生成前确定人物位置、景别、运镜、光线及不同镜头之间的衔接方式。

04

Visual Generation 视觉生成

Generated character references, environments, keyframes, and cinematic scenes based on the visual direction. 根据统一视觉设定生成角色参考图、场景图、首尾帧及电影化镜头画面。

05

AI Video Production AI视频制作

Converted static keyframes into short video sequences with controlled character actions and camera movement. 将静态关键帧转化为短视频片段，并控制人物动作、镜头运动及场景变化。

06

Editing 后期剪辑

Combined generated clips, dialogue, sound effects, pacing, and transitions into complete episodes. 将生成片段、对白、环境音效、节奏及转场整合为完整剧集。

Script & Storyboard 剧本与分镜

Each scene begins with a written script that defines the narrative purpose, character emotions, dialogue, and environment. The script is then divided into shorter shots, with clear instructions for composition, camera movement, timing, lighting, and character positioning. 每个场景首先通过文字剧本确定剧情目的、人物情绪、对白及环境，再将内容拆分为多个短镜头，进一步明确构图、运镜、时长、光线和人物位置。

Character Design 角色设计

The visual identities of Lin Xia and Lu Chuan were established before episode production. Their facial features, hairstyles, clothing, proportions, and emotional expressions were defined through reference images to maintain continuity across different scenes. 在正式制作剧集前，项目首先确定林夏与陆川的角色视觉设定。通过角色参考图统一两人的五官、发型、服装、身形比例及情绪表现，使人物在不同场景和镜头中保持连续性。

World & Scene Design 世界观与场景设计

The Second World is designed as a functioning futuristic city without ordinary residents. Trains, public screens, automated systems, and buildings continue to operate, creating a contrast between an advanced urban environment and the absence of human life. Repeated locations establish a recognizable visual system throughout the series. 第二世界被设定为一座仍在运行、却没有普通居民的未来城市。列车、公共屏幕、自动化系统和建筑设施保持运作，先进城市环境与人类缺席形成强烈反差。项目通过重复出现的核心场景，逐步建立统一且具有辨识度的视觉体系。
