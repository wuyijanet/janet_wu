Editorial Design / Travel Publication

Travel Boook

This project is a travel publication design that explores the cultures, landscapes, landmarks, and natural features of the seven continents. The book uses modern layout design, typography, photography, and a nature-inspired color palette to create an immersive and visually engaging reading experience.

Project Highlights

  • Designed a complete travel publication based on the theme of the seven continents.
  • Used a modern publication style to combine travel research, images, and layout design.
  • Created a blue-toned mountain cover to express calmness, nature, and exploration.
  • Designed different chapter layouts to reflect the unique culture and geography of each continent.
  • Used hyperlinks in the table of contents to make the book easier to navigate.

Design Process

The project began with the goal of creating a modern travel book that could present the diversity of global cultures and landmarks. I wanted the book to be visually appealing, informative, and easy to read.

For the cover, I used a full-coverage blue mountain image as the background. This choice creates a clean and peaceful feeling while also connecting with the themes of travel, nature, sky, and sea.

For the inner pages, I designed each continent with a slightly different layout and color direction. For example, the Asia section uses reds and golds, while the South America section uses greens and yellows. These color choices help show the different natural and cultural features of each continent.

Overall, the final book combines typography, photography, color, and layout design to create an engaging global travel experience for readers.
