Rebirth: Becoming the Villain in Grimm’s Fairy Tales

A group short film reimagining Snow White through a modern perspective. Transported into a fairy-tale world as the Evil Queen, Emma must become the most beautiful woman to return home. Her encounters with an independent Snow White challenge her assumptions about beauty, rivalry, and the role of a princess. 一部以现代视角重新演绎《白雪公主》的小组剧情短片。 Emma 意外进入童话世界，成为母后， 必须完成“成为世界上最美丽的女人”的任务才能回到现实。 然而，拥有独立思想的白雪公主， 逐渐改变了她对美、竞争与公主身份的理解。

Story & Creative Direction 故事与创作方向

  • A Familiar Story, Reversed: Emma follows the Queen’s familiar tactics — a girdle, a comb, and a poisoned apple — but discovers that this Snow White recognizes manipulation and makes her own choices. 经典故事的反转： Emma 尝试使用束腰、梳子和毒苹果完成任务， 却发现眼前的白雪公主能够识别套路， 并坚持自己的判断与选择。

  • A Contemporary Princess: Everyday details, including a burger and bubble tea, bring Snow White into the present. Her direct addresses to the camera give her a voice beyond the original tale. 现代公主的形象： 汉堡、奶茶等日常细节将白雪公主带入现代语境。 她直接面对镜头表达想法， 不再只是原故事中等待他人安排的角色。

  • Rethinking Beauty: The story shifts from competing over appearance to recognizing kindness, courage, and self-worth, replacing rivalry with understanding. 重新理解美： 故事从外貌比较转向对善良、勇气与自我价值的认识， 让角色之间的竞争逐渐转变为理解。

Creative & Production Process 创作与制作过程

  1. Screenplay Development: The screenplay establishes Emma’s everyday life before introducing the fairy-tale world and its system. Three encounters with Snow White build repetition and escalation, leading to a conversation that reframes the meaning of the task. 剧本开发： 剧本先建立 Emma 的现实生活，再引入童话世界与系统任务。 通过与白雪公主的三次相遇， 形成重复与递进的情节结构， 最后以两人的对话重新解释任务的含义。

  2. Storyboarding: The storyboards translate the script into a visual sequence, planning framing, character positions, reactions, and transitions. Wide shots establish locations, while closer shots draw attention to expressions and important objects. 分镜设计： 将文字剧本转化为连续画面， 提前规划构图、人物位置、反应镜头与场景衔接。 通过远景交代环境， 再用近景与特写突出人物表情和关键道具。

  3. Shot Planning: The shot list breaks scenes into individual setups, specifying shot size, camera movement, location, cast, sound requirements, and estimated setup and shooting time. This provides a shared production guide for coordinating the team. 镜头与拍摄规划： 镜头清单将场景拆分为具体拍摄单元， 标明景别、运镜、地点、出镜角色、收音需求， 以及预计布置与拍摄时间， 为团队协作提供统一的执行依据。

  4. Locations, Props & Equipment: The production plan connects campus, park, and home settings to the story’s two worlds. The book, mirror, girdle, comb, and apple basket serve as narrative cues. Listed equipment includes a Sony ZV-E10, tripod, microphone, and shot-specific support such as a gimbal, lighting, and reflector. 场景、道具与设备： 拍摄规划以校园、公园和居家空间承载现实与童话世界。 书本、镜子、束腰、梳子和苹果篮推动情节发展。 设备清单包括 Sony ZV-E10、三脚架与麦克风， 并根据镜头需求安排稳定器、灯光及反光板。

  5. Performance & Camera Language: Dialogue and reaction shots reveal the contrast between Emma’s expectations and Snow White’s responses. Direct-to-camera moments express Snow White’s awareness, while close-ups make the props and emotional turning points visually prominent. 表演与镜头表达： 通过对话和反应镜头， 展现 Emma 的预期与白雪公主实际回应之间的反差。 面向镜头的表达突出白雪公主的自主意识， 道具与表情特写则强化故事线索及情绪转折。

  6. Editing & Sound Structure: The script and shot plan provide a framework for assembling dialogue, reactions, and transitions. The system’s voice-over establishes the game rules, while planned fades and dissolves connect reality, the fairy-tale world, and Emma’s awakening. 剪辑与声音结构： 根据剧本和镜头规划组织对话、人物反应与场景转换。 系统旁白负责交代游戏规则， 淡入淡出与叠化等转场设计， 则连接现实、童话世界及 Emma 醒来的段落。
