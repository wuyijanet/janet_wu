Sound / Audio Essay 声音创作 / 音频随笔

Listening by the Lake: An Audio Essay 湖畔聆听：音频随笔

An audio essay combining my English narration with self-recorded lakeside sounds to explore soundscapes, attention, and everyday listening. The piece brings personal observations into conversation with ideas about sound, media, and environment. 一部结合本人英文旁白与湖畔实地录音的音频随笔， 探索声音景观、注意力与日常聆听。 作品将个人的听觉观察与声音、媒介及环境相关概念相结合。

Listen to the Audio Essay 收听作品

English narration with field recordings. Headphones are recommended for the quieter details. 英文旁白，结合实地环境录音。 建议佩戴耳机，聆听更细微的声音。

Creative Process 创作过程

  • Listening & Field Recording: Recorded the lakeside environment and paid attention to how different sounds moved between the foreground and background of the listening experience. 聆听与实地录音： 记录湖畔的声音环境， 关注不同声音如何在听觉体验中成为焦点或退入背景。

  • Writing & Reflection: Developed an English script connecting personal listening observations with course concepts about soundscapes, attention, and environment. 文本写作与思考： 撰写英文旁白，将个人聆听观察与课程中关于 声音景观、注意力及环境的概念相联系。

  • Voice & Sound: Combined my recorded narration with the field recordings, using voice to guide the discussion and environmental sound to bring listeners into the setting. 旁白与声音组织： 将本人录制的英文旁白与实地录音结合， 以人声引导思考，以环境声呈现具体的聆听场景。
