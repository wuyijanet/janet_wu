Design Process

The project began with the goal of introducing Iceland’s unique natural environment through an engaging digital publication. Rather than creating a traditional PDF book, I chose the EPUB format to provide a more interactive reading experience and allow readers to explore the content in a flexible way.

During the design process, I researched Iceland’s landscapes, wildlife, volcanoes, glaciers, waterfalls, and ecosystems. The content was organized into thematic chapters to help readers navigate different aspects of the country while maintaining a clear and coherent structure.

To enhance user engagement, I incorporated multimedia elements including videos, audio recordings, hyperlinks, and interactive navigation. These features allow readers to experience Iceland beyond static images and text, creating a more immersive digital storytelling experience.

The visual design focuses on large photography, clean typography, and consistent layouts inspired by Iceland’s natural environment. Gestalt design principles such as similarity, contrast, and grouping were applied to improve readability and guide users through the content effectively.

The final outcome is an interactive EPUB publication that combines editorial design, multimedia content, and user-centered design principles to create an engaging exploration of Iceland’s natural beauty and wildlife.
