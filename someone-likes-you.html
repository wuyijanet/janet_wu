Video & Sound / Visual Storytelling 影像与声音 / 视觉叙事

Someone Likes You

An image and sound project following a toy dog's little Valentine's Day adventure. Hoping to surprise its owner, it heads out for the day and returns with a rose and a soda-can ring pull — a small gift with a heartfelt meaning. 一个结合影像与声音的叙事作品，记录玩偶小狗的情人节冒险。 为了给主人准备惊喜，它独自出门， 最后带回一朵玫瑰和一枚易拉罐拉环， 用简单的礼物表达心意。

Story & Concept 故事与创作概念

The story approaches Valentine's Day through the perspective of a toy dog. Its search for a surprise turns an ordinary day out into a small act of affection. A rose and a ring pull become symbols of care, suggesting that the meaning of a gift comes from the thought behind it. 故事从玩偶小狗的视角出发，将一次普通的外出， 变成寻找情人节惊喜的小小旅程。 玫瑰与易拉罐拉环成为表达关心的信物， 也让故事传递出：礼物的意义来自准备它的心意。

Watch the Film 观看作品

Follow a little adventure, from a day out to a Valentine's surprise. 从独自出门到带回礼物，跟随小狗完成这场情人节冒险。

Creative Process 创作过程

  • Story Planning: Developed the toy dog's Valentine's Day adventure, connecting its departure, search for gifts, and return home. 故事策划： 围绕玩偶小狗的情人节冒险， 安排出门、寻找礼物与回家的故事顺序。

  • Photography: Used a Sony ZV-E10 to capture different settings, using toy placement, camera angles, and framing to suggest movement and intention. 影像拍摄： 使用 Sony ZV-E10 拍摄， 通过玩偶摆放、拍摄角度与构图， 表现小狗的行动与意图。

  • Editing: Assembled the material in Adobe Premiere Pro, adjusting image order and shot duration to build the narrative rhythm. 后期剪辑： 使用 Adobe Premiere Pro 整理素材， 调整画面顺序与镜头时长，建立叙事节奏。

  • Sound & Image: Coordinated sound with the images to support transitions and the story's emotional tone. 声音与画面： 配合画面安排声音，衔接场景转换， 营造故事的情绪与氛围。

Selected Photographs 拍摄照片选集

Six selected photographs showing the story's settings, framing, and details. 精选六张拍摄照片，展示故事中的场景、构图与细节。
